IFRS 18, the upcoming IFRS Accounting Standard: what you need to know

kpmg.com

IFRS 18, published April 2024, replaces IAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements. It introduces significant changes to the structure of the income statement and related disclosures to improve comparability of financial performance. The standard does not alter recognition or measurement requirement...

  • Income and expenses classified into three defined categories: operating, investing, and financing.
  • Two new mandatory subtotals: 'Operating profit or loss' and 'Profit or loss before financing and income tax'.
  • Disclosure requirements for management-defined performance measures (MPMs) — subtotals used in public communications to convey management's view of performance.
